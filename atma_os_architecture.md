# AtmaOS Architecture, at a Glance

[← The story behind AtmaOS](/atma_os.html)

## atmaOS

**The brain: a model-independent, agentic cognitive operating system.** It maintains persistent knowledge, identity, memory, governance, and institutional experience across specialized AI systems.

Five things it keeps, across specialized AI systems, whichever model is doing the work:

- **Knowledge:** persistent, not per-chat
- **Identity:** a voice that stays yours
- **Memory:** what, when, and why
- **Governance:** rules for what's approved
- **Institutional experience:** decisions and results on record

## 1. One brain and two branches

atmaOS sits at the center. Two branches stand on the same shared foundation: knowledge, identity, memory, governance, and experience.

### Branch one: the author branch (the knowledge architecture)
Focuses on my books, research, ideas, and editorial work: developing my thinking, finding connections across decades of writing, and improving drafts without losing my voice.

### Branch two: the agency (the marketing architecture)
Uses the same shared foundation to support AI product managers, designers, copywriters, developers, advertising experts, and analysts. They draw on approved knowledge, work together on projects, and build a lasting record of their decisions and results. See [Growth Bastards](https://growthbastards.com/).

## 2. Inside the agency: fifteen personas, always learning

The agency is [Growth Bastards](https://growthbastards.com/): fifteen AI specialists led by one human CMO. They are built from three streams of knowledge, which keep them current and keep them sharp.

- **Curated knowledge sets:** approved material each persona draws on.
- **Ongoing academic studies:** continuing research, folded into what they know.
- **"Listening" skills:** learning from conversation happening online right now, staying abreast of up-to-the-minute breakthroughs and thinking.

## 3. The engines

Specialized engines plug into AtmaOS. Either division can use any of them.

- **Ernest-Faulkner** (editorial). Editorial judgment, from a machine with a human editor's mindset. [Read how it works](/ernest-faulkner).
- **Boswell Engine** (linguistic and semantic). A linguistic and semantic scraper that reads people through their words. [boswell.atma.marketing](https://boswell.atma.marketing/). Four jobs:
  1. **Profile and reach B2B buyers.** Builds evidence-based psychological and linguistic profiles from public data, then generates outreach tailored to each buyer's decision-making style, in the sender's voice.
  2. **Catalogue experts.** Builds in-depth canonical catalogues of experts, to build into the marketing personas.
  3. **Understand B2C users.** Analyses existing customer lists into in-depth cognitive and mindset profiles, to help identify and target future users.
  4. **Prepare for the room.** Profiles a specific target to prepare me (or anyone) for sales calls, pitches, or interviews.
- **Assumption Annihilator** (founders, pre-campaign). Helps early-stage founders prioritize the key assumptions that must be proven or disproven for the enterprise to succeed. Not used every time, but sometimes required before a campaign even starts. Already built.
- **CIE, the Creative Intelligence Engine** (a dual-system extension to atmaOS). Data-driven creative planning. Two systems work in sequence: the first learns what works, the second decides what to do about it.
  - **Creative Evidence Engine.** A continually updated knowledge base. Ingests and scores performance data and research on creative assets (video, images, etc.), classifies them by format and style, and quantifies their impact with uncertainty.
  - **Creative Decision Engine.** A campaign-specific optimizer. Uses that knowledge plus campaign context (objectives, audience, constraints) to rank feasible creative approaches, estimate their ROI distributions, and recommend experiments or deployment to maximize expected value.
  - Subset tools: **The Marketing Opportunity Atlas** (a comprehensive audit, research, and classification of marketing strategies, channels, tactics, and growth opportunities) and **Bayesian Statistical Ad Management** (connects to any client's ad management dashboards and provides real-time analysis).
- **The User Adoption Simulation Engine** (still to come). The inverse of the Boswell. Creates psychodynamic personas at scale, so new products or features can be tested for emotional appeal across different target audiences.

## 4. The next phase

- **Machine learning for client data.** Dashboards, algorithms, and warehousing for assessing client data.
- **Ad and content creation at scale.** An engine for managing ad and content creation when done at scale.
