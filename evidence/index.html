Boswell Engine

Boswell turns attributable language into a structured account of expression, stated reasoning, and recurring patterns. Every admitted finding connects to its source; ambiguous interpretations go to review.

The result is usable intelligence for buyer research, expert knowledge, and important conversations. Its public study lets you compare what the same AI writer produced with and without that research.
